# Alpha 4 Known-Word Authority and Cache Stabilization

Alpha 4 separates Anki-derived and manual-known ownership while exposing a deliberate effective union. Cache authority reports unavailable, loading, ready, partial, or failed state together with completeness, refresh time, source counts, and failures.

A word is confirmed unknown only when the Anki cache is ready and complete and the word is absent from both Anki and manual-known sets. Missing, partial, or failed cache state is indeterminate and is not projected into New Words as confirmed unknown.

Known-word mutations continue to consume the Alpha 2 canonical Reader interaction identity. Manual removal never removes Anki ownership. JP Analyzer, Teaching, EPUB, contextual scenes, enrichment, dictionaries, prefetch, Yomitan independence, and latest-note behavior are unchanged.
