# Phase 15.9A Reader chrome compaction
Phase 15.9A removes repeated book, chapter, scene, and progress information from the status strip, sidebar, and navigation card. Book identity and reading progress remain in the Reader header; chapter selection remains in the reading sidebar; Previous, scene jump, and Next remain in the compact navigation row.

The sidebar toggle moves to an edge rail and, on narrow screens, becomes a fixed close control that does not cover sidebar content. Reader chrome, padding, and empty action-area height are reduced so the book viewport receives more vertical space without reducing Japanese text size.

EPUB reconstruction, Reader spans, known-word state, mining, Teaching, dictionaries, persistence, Debug Report, JP Analyzer contracts, backend production behavior, and launchers are unchanged.
